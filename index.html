Can I use Hedgehog offline?

Yes. You can access and manage your stored information without network connectivity. An internet connection is needed for save, restore and sync operations.

What can I store in Hedgehog?

Hedgehog keeps login credentials, credit card details and private notes together in one encrypted place on your Android device.

Can you see my passwords?

No. Hedgehog uses a zero-knowledge system, so we cannot read the secured data you store on your device or on our server. You can read more in our privacy policy.

What if I forget my master password?

Your master password is used to generate your encryption key, and we cannot recover it. If you forget it, you will lose access to your secured data. Choose a strong master password you can remember and keep it safe.

How do save, restore and sync work?

Hedgehog can save, restore or sync encrypted data between your Android device and our Google Firebase server. These operations require an internet connection. The app includes in-app purchases; check Google Play and the app for current options.
